Hello, I'm Ella.

I notice the things people stop noticing.

I’m most interested in the small moments that quietly shape how a product feels. An extra step, an unclear choice, or a workaround people accept simply because they have gotten used to it. I use research and observation to find these overlooked problems,
then rethink them through digital experiences that fit more naturally into everyday life.

I’m especially interested in how digital design can make real interactions easier without demanding more attention from the user. Currently studying Communication Design and HCI at Washington University in St. Louis.
